# The Forbidden Gates — Canonical Game Bible

## High concept
A cinematic third-person fantasy action-adventure RPG about a young royal scout who crosses the forbidden borders of the kingdom of Elaris after Princess Elyra Vale vanishes with an invading army. The rescue journey becomes a mystery about the ancient Aetherians, the Gate Masters, the origin of magic, and the truth hidden beneath Elaris itself.

## Tone
Epic, magical, mysterious, adventurous and emotionally serious without relying on graphic violence. The world should alternate between beauty and danger: luminous forests, impossible sky ruins, submerged temples, volcanic kingdoms, dream realms and ancient gate structures.

## Core player fantasy
Become an unknown traveler who learns to read and command ancient Gate magic, grows into a legendary explorer, and ultimately decides the fate of every realm connected by the Forbidden Gates.

## Main characters
### Kael Ardyn
The default protagonist. A young royal scout who survives the fall of Elaris. Kael begins capable but unimportant. During the invasion, an ancient Gate responds to him, revealing a connection nobody understands. Player-facing character customization can alter appearance while keeping Kael's canonical role and dialogue identity.

### Princess Elyra Vale
Heir to Elaris. Intelligent, curious and secretly knowledgeable about the ancient Gates. She is not a passive objective. Her decisions shape the second half of the campaign. Her bloodline carries Gatefire, an Aetherian inheritance capable of awakening or sealing the oldest Gates.

### King Aldren Vale
Ruler of Elaris and Elyra's father. He has hidden parts of the kingdom's history to prevent panic and protect the royal bloodline. His secrets become central to the conflict.

### Emperor Vaelor Veyr
Leader of the Veyr Dominion. Initially presented as the main enemy, but his true goal is to prevent the Heart Gate from opening. He is ruthless in his methods but not wrong about the danger beneath Elaris.

### Orion, Master of the First Gate
The oldest surviving Gate Master. He knows what happened to the Aetherians and why Kael can interact with Gate structures.

### Vael, the Fallen Gate Master
A once-honored guardian corrupted by Hollow influence. Vael becomes a recurring rival and a warning about what unchecked Gate power can do.

## Ancient history
The Aetherians discovered a form of magic called Aether and learned to construct Gates that folded distance between distant realms. Their network transformed civilization, but exploration eventually exposed an extradimensional force called the Hollow. The Hollow consumes and distorts magic.

The Aetherians sealed the network, created the Gate Masters, and built the Heart Gate beneath what later became Elaris. The Heart Gate is not a doorway to another kingdom; it is the primary seal containing the First Hollow.

Over centuries, history became myth. Elaris grew above the seal while its rulers inherited fragments of Aetherian knowledge.

## Inciting event
The Veyr Dominion attacks Elaris. Its forces ignore most territorial objectives and focus on the royal palace. Princess Elyra disappears with them beyond the forbidden border. Elaris assumes she has been kidnapped. Kael is sent after her because the nearest dormant Gate unexpectedly recognizes him.

## Midpoint revelation
Around Level 50, Kael reaches Elyra. She refuses to simply return home. She reveals that the Dominion has shown her evidence that the Heart Gate is failing and that Elaris has hidden the truth for generations. From this point onward, Kael and Elyra travel together intermittently while investigating both sides.

## Final revelation
The Gates were not created to keep dangerous outsiders away from Elaris. The surviving Gate network was deliberately arranged to keep the First Hollow sealed beneath Elaris. Kael's connection comes from a forgotten Gate Master lineage designed to act as a living failsafe if the royal Gatefire bloodline failed.

## Ending framework
Player choices across the campaign influence relationships, optional quests and the final decision.

### Seal ending
Kael and Elyra permanently reinforce the Heart Gate. Inter-realm Gate travel ends and magic slowly becomes rarer, but the immediate threat is contained.

### Open realms ending
The Heart Gate is destroyed while the First Hollow is defeated, leaving the surviving realms permanently connected without the old Gate controls.

### Gate Master ending
Kael becomes the new Master of the Heart Gate, preserving travel while accepting lifelong responsibility for the network.

### Secret ending — The Eighth Gate
If the player completes the hidden Gate sigils and resolves key Gate Master stories, a Gate unknown to both Aetherians and Orion appears after the ending. It opens into an unmapped realm, establishing a sequel hook without invalidating the chosen ending.

# World structure
The campaign contains ten chapters of ten substantial levels each. Each chapter introduces a realm identity, traversal idea, magical mechanic, enemy ecosystem, side-story thread and chapter climax.

## Chapter 1 — The Fall of Elaris
Purpose: establish characters, controls, kingdom culture, invasion, first magic contact and the decision to cross the border.

## Chapter 2 — The Whispering Wilds
Purpose: establish that the outside world is alive, populated and more complex than Elarian stories claimed.

## Chapter 3 — The Ember Kingdom
Purpose: first major Gate Master trial and the discovery that some Gate Masters distrust Elaris.

## Chapter 4 — The Sunken Realm
Purpose: reveal Aetherian ruins and the first strong evidence that official history is incomplete.

## Chapter 5 — The Stormlands
Purpose: reach Elyra and overturn the original rescue premise.

## Chapter 6 — The Broken Kingdom
Purpose: travel with Elyra, investigate Veyr motives and expose political consequences of the truth.

## Chapter 7 — The Shadow Realm
Purpose: Hollow corruption becomes a direct world-scale threat.

## Chapter 8 — The Ancient World
Purpose: discover surviving Aetherian records, Kael's lineage and the construction of the Gate network.

## Chapter 9 — The Last Gate
Purpose: alliances collapse and reform as Elaris, Veyr, Gate Masters and corrupted forces converge.

## Chapter 10 — The Heart Gate
Purpose: return to Elaris, descend beneath the kingdom and resolve the central mystery.

# Magic system
Magic is built around learnable Aether disciplines rather than fixed character classes.

## Disciplines
- Ember: heat, ignition and area pressure
- Tide: water shaping, cleansing and mobility
- Frost: slowing, barriers and environmental control
- Storm: lightning, wind and rapid movement
- Verdant: roots, growth and restoration
- Radiant: light, wards and revelation of hidden objects
- Umbral: concealment, displacement and shadow traversal
- Spirit: protection, recovery and interaction with memories
- Rune: traps, seals, switches and Gate mechanisms
- Gatefire: rare story-linked magic used for Gate control

## Combination philosophy
Players can equip a small active loadout while retaining a larger learned library. Compatible disciplines can produce combo effects. Combos should be authored as game rules, not arbitrary physics, so they remain readable and balanceable on both desktop and mobile.

# Core gameplay loop
1. Enter a level and receive a story or exploration objective.
2. Explore a large handcrafted environment with optional branches.
3. Solve traversal, environmental and magical puzzles.
4. Encounter enemies and use movement, magic, companions and defensive timing.
5. Discover lore, collectibles, upgrades and side quests.
6. Reach a major encounter, Gate trial or story scene.
7. Unlock a checkpoint, ability, relationship beat or route to the next level.

# Progression
- Character level improves survivability and resource capacity.
- Discipline mastery unlocks spell variants and utility options.
- Gate Sigils unlock ancient traversal and secret areas.
- Relics modify play style without invalidating earlier equipment.
- Companion bonds unlock dialogue, support actions and alternate quest outcomes.
- Realm completion unlocks challenge versions and lore archives.

# Boss philosophy
Bosses are spectacle encounters built around readable patterns, movement, magical counters and environmental mechanics. Every chapter climax should test the mechanic introduced by that realm rather than only increasing health.

# Difficulty
- Story: generous timing, stronger guidance and reduced encounter pressure.
- Adventurer: intended default.
- Gate Master: tighter resources and advanced encounter behavior.
- Custom: independent assists for timing windows, navigation, puzzle hints and incoming pressure.

# Accessibility requirements
- Full subtitles with speaker labels and size controls
- Remappable desktop controls
- Customizable touch controls on Android
- Hold/toggle choices for repeated inputs
- Adjustable camera shake and motion blur
- Color-independent symbols for puzzles and status effects
- Aim and target assistance options
- Puzzle hint progression
- Scalable HUD and text
- Separate music, effects, dialogue and ambience controls

# Content rules
- No realm should be visually interchangeable with another.
- Every ten-level chapter must contain at least one major settlement or safe hub, one optional lore route, one traversal set piece, one major Gate interaction and one chapter climax.
- Long levels should use checkpoints and streaming zones instead of forcing full restarts.
- Story-critical information cannot exist only in collectibles.
- Mobile optimization must never remove required clues or gameplay mechanics; it may reduce visual complexity only.

# Canonical title
**The Forbidden Gates**

Optional release subtitle for the first game: **The Forbidden Gates: Beyond Elaris**.
