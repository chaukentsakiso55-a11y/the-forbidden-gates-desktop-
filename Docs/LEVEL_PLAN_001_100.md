# The Forbidden Gates — 100-Level Campaign Plan

Each level is intended to be substantial, checkpointed, replayable, and built from multiple streaming sections rather than a single corridor. Optional exploration routes, lore, challenges and secrets are layered onto the critical path.

## Chapter I — The Fall of Elaris
1. **Morning in Elaris** — Explore the capital, meet Elyra, learn movement and interaction, and establish the royal festival.
2. **The Sky Turns Red** — The Veyr invasion begins; Kael helps civilians reach protected districts.
3. **Palace Under Siege** — Reach the palace through damaged streets and learn basic defensive magic.
4. **The Vanishing Princess** — Witness Elyra taken through a dormant Gate that should not be able to open.
5. **Ashes of the Courtyard** — Search for survivors, recover the first Gate Sigil, and learn that the attackers ignored most treasure.
6. **Road to the Border** — Travel through occupied countryside and encounter the first Veyr scouts and displaced villagers.
7. **The Watchtower** — Restore an ancient beacon and discover records about the forbidden border.
8. **Whispers Beneath Stone** — Enter a buried Aetherian ruin where the Gate responds directly to Kael.
9. **Trial of the First Seal** — Solve rune mechanisms and survive the first Gate trial.
10. **Beyond Elaris** — Confront the invasion commander, open the first Forbidden Gate, and cross into the unknown.

## Chapter II — The Whispering Wilds
11. **Forest of Living Light** — Enter a luminous forest where plants react to magic.
12. **Paths That Move** — Learn to read natural rune markers as the forest rearranges routes.
13. **Village in the Canopy** — Meet outsiders who know Elaris only through old warnings.
14. **The Hollow Tree** — Explore an enormous living tree containing an ancient shrine.
15. **Song of the Moss Spirits** — Use Spirit and Verdant interactions to restore a damaged sanctuary.
16. **The Hunter's Moon** — Track a corrupted magical creature and discover Hollow residue.
17. **Roots of Memory** — Enter a memory grove that shows fragments of the Aetherian collapse.
18. **The Thorn Maze** — Navigate a shifting magical labyrinth with optional relic chambers.
19. **Guardian of Roots** — Reach the forest Gate and learn that a Gate Master once vanished here.
20. **The Verdant Seal** — Complete the realm trial, cleanse the Gate, and unlock Verdant mastery.

## Chapter III — The Ember Kingdom
21. **Road of Cinders** — Enter volcanic highlands and manage heat vents as traversal hazards.
22. **City Beneath the Volcano** — Reach a forge-city built around flowing magical channels.
23. **The Ember Accord** — Meet factions divided over whether outsiders should be allowed through the Gates.
24. **Temple of Burning Glass** — Explore a reflective obsidian temple and solve light-and-heat puzzles.
25. **River of Firelight** — Cross moving platforms above glowing mineral rivers using Rune mechanisms.
26. **The Sleeping Furnace** — Reactivate an ancient Aetherian power chamber.
27. **Seraphon's Challenge** — First encounter with Seraphon, Master of Flame, who refuses passage.
28. **The Crown of Embers** — Recover a ceremonial relic proving Elaris once negotiated with the Gate Masters.
29. **Arena of the Gate Master** — Complete Seraphon's multi-stage trial focused on timing and environmental control.
30. **Oath of Flame** — Earn Seraphon's respect and learn that King Aldren knows more than he admitted.

## Chapter IV — The Sunken Realm
31. **Shore of Silent Bells** — Arrive at a drowned coast where towers rise from the sea.
32. **The Breathing Temple** — Gain magical underwater traversal within sealed air corridors.
33. **City Below the Waves** — Explore a submerged civilization and meet its surviving descendants.
34. **Library of Tides** — Recover Aetherian records preserved inside water-locked archives.
35. **The Drowned Observatory** — Align ancient celestial instruments to locate Elyra's route.
36. **Choir of the Deep** — Follow magical sound cues through dark flooded chambers.
37. **The Broken Leviathan Shrine** — Restore a colossal guardian monument to open the lower city.
38. **Tidekeeper's Secret** — Learn that the Heart Gate was designed as a containment seal.
39. **Palace of the Drowned King** — Navigate a collapsing royal ruin as Hollow corruption spreads.
40. **The Ocean Gate** — Defeat the chapter threat, restore the Gate, and unlock advanced Tide magic.

## Chapter V — The Stormlands
41. **Islands Above the Clouds** — Enter floating islands linked by wind currents and broken bridges.
42. **Lightning Road** — Learn Storm traversal and redirect lightning into dormant machinery.
43. **Citadel of Winds** — Reach a sky settlement controlled by rival navigators.
44. **The Falling Archive** — Explore a library drifting apart in the storm.
45. **Tharos Awakens** — Meet Tharos, Keeper of Storms, who believes Elyra willingly crossed the realm.
46. **Eye of the Tempest** — Travel through the calm center of a supernatural storm and discover Veyr camps.
47. **The Sky Prison** — Infiltrate a fortress where important Aetherian researchers are being held.
48. **Bridge of Thunder** — Unite local factions to activate a route to the Dominion convoy.
49. **The Dominion's Truth** — Find evidence that the Veyr are trying to prevent the Heart Gate from opening.
50. **Elyra** — Kael finally reaches the princess. She refuses to return to Elaris and reveals that she has been investigating the failing seal.

## Chapter VI — The Broken Kingdom
51. **An Uneasy Alliance** — Travel with Elyra while both sides distrust each other.
52. **Ruins of Veyr** — Discover that the Dominion's homeland was damaged by earlier Gate failures.
53. **The Emperor's Message** — Meet Emperor Vaelor indirectly and hear his version of the ancient history.
54. **City of Two Banners** — Resolve conflict in a divided settlement caught between Veyr and Elarian influence.
55. **The Glass Frontier** — Cross a crystalline desert where Gate energy creates mirages.
56. **Echoes of the Throne** — Elyra discovers records left by previous Elarian monarchs.
57. **The Betrayer's Road** — A trusted ally steals a Gate Sigil, forcing pursuit through abandoned forts.
58. **Citadel of Fractures** — Enter a fortress split by unstable portals.
59. **The King's Secret** — Learn that Aldren has been searching for a way to control the Heart Gate rather than merely seal it.
60. **No Road Home** — Kael and Elyra choose to continue toward the ancient world instead of returning to either army.

## Chapter VII — The Shadow Realm
61. **Where Light Fails** — Enter a realm where ordinary light cannot reveal every path.
62. **The City of Echoes** — Explore a silent city populated by memory projections.
63. **Umbral Passage** — Learn shadow traversal and use it to cross impossible architecture.
64. **The Nameless Chapel** — Discover records of the first Gate Masters sacrificing their identities to protect the network.
65. **Hollow Bloom** — Witness corruption spreading through magic itself.
66. **The Mirror Enemy** — Face magical reflections that imitate the player's equipped abilities.
67. **Vael the Fallen** — Meet the corrupted Gate Master who claims Kael will eventually become like him.
68. **Labyrinth of Doubt** — Navigate illusions built from Kael's memories and fears.
69. **The Broken Master** — Confront Vael and learn how the Hollow corrupts Gate bonds.
70. **Shadow Seal** — Stabilize the realm Gate, but discover that the Heart Gate has begun opening on its own.

## Chapter VIII — The Ancient World
71. **Aetheria's Edge** — Arrive in the ruins of the civilization that built the Gates.
72. **The Machine Garden** — Restore living magical machinery that has continued operating for centuries.
73. **Hall of Names** — Discover the original Gate Master lineages.
74. **Kael Ardyn** — Reveal that Kael descends from the dormant Heart Gate failsafe lineage.
75. **Elyra's Fire** — Elyra learns to control Gatefire rather than merely trigger it.
76. **The First Network** — Explore a hub once capable of opening routes to hundreds of realms.
77. **Orion** — Meet the Master of the First Gate and hear the complete history of the Hollow war.
78. **The Lost Gate Masters** — Recover records showing that several guardians survived in hidden realms.
79. **Heart Key** — Assemble the components needed to reach the Heart Gate beneath Elaris.
80. **The Ancient Choice** — Decide which alliances to trust before the final return journey begins.

## Chapter IX — The Last Gate
81. **War at the Crossroads** — Elarian and Veyr armies collide around the surviving Gate network.
82. **The Gate Masters Gather** — Recruit or confront guardians based on earlier decisions.
83. **Fall of the Sky Route** — Escape a collapsing inter-realm passage as the network destabilizes.
84. **The Emperor in Person** — Kael and Elyra finally meet Vaelor and negotiate or reject a temporary alliance.
85. **Return Through Fire** — Revisit the Ember Kingdom under Hollow attack.
86. **Forest Without Song** — Return to the Whispering Wilds and see the consequences of spreading corruption.
87. **The Last Sigil** — Recover the final required seal from a hidden Aetherian vault.
88. **Vael's Redemption** — Resolve the Fallen Gate Master's story, with outcomes based on prior choices.
89. **The Gate Before Home** — Reach the final exterior Gate as Elaris appears beyond it.
90. **The Last Gate Opens** — Cross home with allies while the Heart Gate's energy transforms the capital.

## Chapter X — The Heart Gate
91. **Elaris Changed** — Return to a capital filled with unstable Gate phenomena and competing forces.
92. **The Empty Palace** — Search the abandoned royal palace for Aldren and the Heart Gate route.
93. **Below the Throne** — Descend into Aetherian structures far older than the kingdom.
94. **The King's Decision** — Confront Aldren about his attempt to control the Heart Gate.
95. **Vault of Gatefire** — Elyra unlocks the royal bloodline archive and learns the final sealing ritual.
96. **The First Seal** — Restore the outer mechanisms while the First Hollow influences the environment.
97. **Master of the Heart** — Kael activates his inherited Gate Master bond.
98. **The Heart Gate** — Enter the vast central Gate chamber and choose which allied plan to follow.
99. **The First Hollow** — Final multi-phase magical encounter built around every major discipline and Gate mechanic learned across the campaign.
100. **What Lies Beyond** — Resolve the fate of the Gates, show the consequences of major choices, unlock endgame exploration, and reveal the Eighth Gate for players who completed the secret requirements.
